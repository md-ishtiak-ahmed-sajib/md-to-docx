## Common Questions

- The application does not require Node.js, an IDE, a database, a driver, or Microsoft Word to convert files.
- Microsoft Word or another DOCX-compatible reader is required only to open the exported document.
- Conversion occurs locally. Remote images supplied in Markdown can require an internet connection.
- Private AI citation markers such as `turn...search...` cannot resolve outside their original session; the compatibility system removes and reports them.

## Response Scope

Support covers installation, application startup, Markdown preview, themes, compatibility diagnostics, and DOCX export. It does not include restoring deleted files, resolving third-party web services, or guaranteeing every third-party Markdown dialect.

Phone numbers and postal addresses are not support channels and are intentionally not published.
