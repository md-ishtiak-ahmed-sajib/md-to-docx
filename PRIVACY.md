# MD to DOCX Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Applies to: MD to DOCX 1.7.0 and later

## Summary

MD to DOCX converts Markdown into DOCX files on your device. The application has no user accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, subscription system, developer-operated database, cloud storage, or application backend.

## Information Processed Locally

Markdown that you type, paste, or open; document metadata; previews; images; theme choices; and generated DOCX files are processed locally. Opened documents are held in application memory while the app is running. Generated files are written only when you choose to download or save them.

The Windows application serves its bundled interface from `127.0.0.1`. This loopback server is reachable only from the same computer, accepts only `GET` and `HEAD`, has no upload endpoint, and does not log or retain document contents.

## Local Preferences

The selected theme is stored in browser-style local storage under the key `markdown-docx-theme` for the application's local origin. No document text is intentionally persisted in local storage. You can remove the preference by clearing the app's site data or uninstalling the app.

## User-Requested External Requests

Markdown can contain remote images and links. When you supply or activate one:

- Preview or DOCX export may request a remote image so it can be displayed or embedded.
- Opening a link, source repository, support page, privacy page, or email address contacts the selected destination.

The external destination may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and request headers. Its own privacy policy governs that request. MD to DOCX does not add tracking identifiers or send your document to the maintainer.

## Microsoft Store Data

Microsoft may process Store acquisition, update, diagnostic, or account information as part of operating Microsoft Windows and Microsoft Store. MD to DOCX does not receive that information through an application-operated service. Microsoft's handling of Store data is governed by the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Retention and Deletion

The maintainer does not receive or retain your documents. You control uploaded source files and exported DOCX files on your device. Close the application to discard in-memory document state, clear the application's local site data to remove the theme preference, and uninstall MD to DOCX through Windows Settings to remove the application.

## Children and Sensitive Information

The application is a general document utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other user. Because user content remains local, you are responsible for deciding what information to place in your documents and where to save or share exported files.

## Changes

This policy will be updated before a release that materially changes data handling. The effective date and applicable version will be revised with that release.
